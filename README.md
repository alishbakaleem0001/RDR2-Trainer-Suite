## 🌟 Why Players Choose cvsTrainer

There's a particular feeling that hits when you realize the world around you can be rearranged on a whim. Maybe it's summoning an impossible carriage to a snowy peak, or teleporting from the swamps of Lemoyne to the cliffs of Ambarino in a single keystroke. cvsTrainer exists to deliver that feeling with the polish of a native feature and the reliability of software that respects your machine.

Where other trainers crash, cvsTrainer quietly hums. Where others bog down the frame rate, cvsTrainer stays light. Where others feel like a bolted-on tool, cvsTrainer blends into the experience like a well-worn leather satchel.

Three qualities separate cvsTrainer from the crowd:

- **Native integration.** The trainer works inside the game's own rendering pipeline, so overlays and menus appear without breaking immersion.
- **Thoughtful design.** Every menu, toggle, and slider sits where your muscle memory expects it.
- **Respect for your time.** The whole system loads quickly, saves your preferences, and lets you get back to the frontier.

## 🧩 Feature Overview

cvsTrainer bundles a sprawling set of capabilities into one cohesive trainer. Below is a map of the major pillars.

### 🎯 Player Toolkit
- Health, stamina, and dead-eye presets that scale on the fly
- Speed modifiers with fine-grained multipliers
- Invisibility, super jump, and no-clip exploration modes
- Weather and time-of-day controls, including frozen time
- Wanted level override and lawman suppression toggles
- Custom player model swapping for experimentation

### 🔫 Weapons & Combat
- Spawn any firearm, throwable, or melee tool from a searchable catalog
- Adjust damage, fire rate, magazine size, and projectile behavior
- Infinite ammunition toggle with per-category granularity
- Attachments, tints, and engravings unlocked for every firearm
- Rapid-fire, no-recoil, and no-reload convenience toggles

### 🐎 Vehicles & Mounts
- Spawn wagons, boats, trains, and novelty vehicles at will
- Summon a personal mount from any distance
- Tune vehicle handling, durability, and speed curves
- Remove unwanted passengers or take control of any carriage
- Save custom vehicle loadouts to reuse later

### 🗺️ World & Teleportation
- Waypoint teleport with an option to preview coordinates
- Preset teleport destinations across every region
- Save up to 64 custom teleport slots
- Under-map and skybox exploration modes
- Entity spawner for props, animals, and NPCs

### 🎨 Native Overlay
- Clean ImGui-based menu with adjustable transparency
- Theming with light and dark palettes
- Keybind rebinding for every action
- On-screen HUD widgets that can be toggled individually
- Fully responsive layout for ultrawide and 4K displays

The menu adapts to your screen like water fills a glass. ## 🚀 Getting Started

Setting up cvsTrainer is one of the fastest parts of your journey. You don't need to wrangle a toolchain or wrestle with a package manager. Instead, the project ships as a compact build that drops straight into place.

**High-level steps:**

1. Obtain the latest release using the download macro provided at the top and bottom of this README.
2. Place the trainer files into the folder where your game executable lives.
3. Launch Red Dead Redemption 2 and wait until you're in the world.
4. Press the default toggle key (F4) to raise the overlay.
5. Wander the menus, tune what you like, and save a profile.

Detailed walkthroughs, GIFs, and troubleshooting notes live in the repository's wiki and issues area.

## 🛠️ Configuration & Profiles

Everything you toggle in cvsTrainer lives in a portable configuration file. Preference profiles let you snap between setups — a stealthy "exploration" preset, a chaotic "sandbox" preset, and a minimal "cinematic" preset are all possible.

Profiles are plain text, human-readable, and easy to share with friends. Copy a file, hand it over, and they'll inherit your arrangement of hotkeys, colors, and defaults.

## 🚂 Vehicle Spawning System

The vehicle spawner is where physics takes a holiday. Boats appear on dry land. Trains materialize on mountain ridges. Carriages hover where no road has ever run. It's absurd in the best possible way, and it's all managed through a clean searchable dropdown.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Will this affect my save files?**
Spawned entities and toggled settings are session-scoped unless you deliberately save. Your campaign saves are not modified by the trainer.

**Does it work with other overlays?**
In most cases, yes. The overlay uses a low-conflict hook that coexists with popular tools. If you notice a clash, report it and we'll investigate.

**Can I disable specific features?**
Absolutely. Every subsystem can be turned off individually, so you can run a minimal configuration if you prefer.

**Is there a risk of crashes?**
The trainer is engineered for stability, and any known issues are tracked in the repository. If something misbehaves, open an issue with a reproduction and log.

**How often is it updated?**
The project follows a rolling release model in 2026, with frequent small updates and occasional larger milestone releases.
